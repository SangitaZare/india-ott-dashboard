How many Indians pay for streaming?

I went looking for one number and found four:
• 664.9M people watch online video
• 216M paid video subscriptions
• …held by just 143M households
• …and only 172.6M of them were actually used last month

None of these is wrong. They count different things.

It gets stranger for a single platform. JioHotstar's "paying subscribers" range from 180M to 300M depending on who you ask: the company, a research firm or a broker.

So I built a data project around it:
📊 A dataset of 48 OTT plans, 21 telecom bundles and 19 subscriber figures, every value linked to its source
🔍 A dashboard that shows why the numbers disagree
💰 A cost model in ₹ per month: paying yearly saves 58–69%
👨‍👩‍👧 A family plan picker that finds the cheapest combination for your screens, languages and budget

The biggest lesson: a good analysis says "unknown" when the data doesn't know.

Code and data: github.com/SangitaZare/india-ott-dashboard
Live dashboard: sangitazare.github.io/india-ott-dashboard
